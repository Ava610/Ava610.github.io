A Quebec classic, a record to beat

Tire sur la neige is hot maple syrup poured onto packed snow, then rolled onto a stick. It is the taste of a Quebec winter.

On the day, 600 students, staff, alumni and neighbours will eat it together, at the same moment, while officials count. If we reach the number, the record is ours.
